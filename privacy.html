Privacy Policy

This Google OAuth client is used solely by its owner to access their own Google Drive accounts through rclone on their own computer.

  • No data is collected, stored, or processed by any server operated for this client.
  • Files accessed via the Google Drive API stay between Google and the owner's computer.
  • No data is shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
